# ScreenDuel Support

ScreenDuel is a daily screen time duel with a friend. Stay under your goal and use less than them to win the day.

## Contact

Questions, bugs or account help: **alperreren.apps@gmail.com**

## Delete your account

Open the app and go to **Profile → Delete Account**. This permanently deletes your account and all your data. You can also email us to request deletion.

## Subscriptions

Premium is billed through the App Store. Manage or cancel it in **Settings → your name → Subscriptions** on your iPhone.
