# ScreenDuel Privacy Policy

_Last updated: November 17, 2026_

ScreenDuel ("we", "the app") helps you and your friends reduce screen time
through friendly competition. Privacy is the core of this product; this policy
explains exactly what we collect, how long we keep it, and what we never
collect.

## What we collect

| Data | Purpose | Where it is stored |
|---|---|---|
| Apple account identifier and email address (may be an Apple private relay address) | Sign in with Apple | Supabase (EU region) |
| Username | Shown to your duel partners | Supabase |
| Timezone | Computing when your duel day ends | Supabase |
| **Total daily screen time (minutes)** | The duel: comparing totals with partners you chose | Supabase |
| Daily goals, results, streaks, achievements | Core app features | Supabase |
| Push notification token | Sending daily results and partner notifications | Supabase / Expo |
| Subscription status | Unlocking Premium features | RevenueCat |

## What we never collect

- **Which apps or websites you use.** Per-app data never leaves your device —
  Apple's Screen Time framework enforces this. Only one number, your total
  daily minutes, is uploaded.
- Your contacts, location, photos, messages, or browsing history.
- Screen time data of anyone who has not created an account and joined a group.

ScreenDuel never blocks, limits or controls apps on your device or anyone
else's. It only measures your own total and compares it with your partners.

## Sharing

Your total daily minutes are visible **only** to members of groups you join
voluntarily. Leave a group at any time to stop sharing (Profile → Leave Duel).
We never sell your data.

Service providers that process data on our behalf: Supabase (database and
authentication), Expo (push notifications), RevenueCat (subscriptions) and
Google AdMob (ads for free accounts).

## Advertising

Free accounts see a single banner ad served by Google AdMob. Premium removes
ads.

## How long we keep data

- **Free accounts and expired subscriptions:** daily screen time and daily
  results are kept for the **last 365 days**; older records are deleted
  automatically.
- **Active Premium:** your full history is kept while the subscription is
  active, so long-term stats and streaks work. When the subscription ends,
  records older than 365 days are deleted.
- **Account data** (username, timezone, achievements) is kept until you delete
  your account.

## Deleting your account

Open **Profile → Delete Account** and confirm. This permanently deletes your
account and all associated data — profile, screen time history, results,
stats, achievements, group memberships and subscription record — and revokes
the app's Sign in with Apple access. Deletion cannot be undone.

If you own a group, ownership passes to another member so their duel
continues. Deleting your account does not cancel an App Store subscription;
cancel it in your App Store account settings.

You can also request deletion by emailing alperreren.apps@gmail.com.

## Children

ScreenDuel is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect
data from children under 13.
